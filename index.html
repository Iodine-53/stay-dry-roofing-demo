/// FAQ

Straight answers.

How much does a roof replacement cost?

$8,900–$18,000 for most Tampa homes, depending on size, pitch, and material — shingle is the floor, metal and tile run higher. The written quote is final. It doesn't move after we start.

Can it be repaired, or does it need replacing?

If the deck is solid and the damage is local — a failed boot, a lifted run — repair wins. If shingles are curling across whole slopes or the roof is past 20 years, replacement is cheaper than chasing leaks. We photograph what we find and tell you straight.

How long does a replacement take?

Most Tampa homes: one day, tear-off to dry-in. Bigger or tile jobs run two to three. You're never uncovered overnight — we dry-in every slope before we leave.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. We document storm damage the way adjusters want it — dated photos, measurements, line items — and we'll meet the adjuster on the roof if you want us there.

What warranty do I get?

10-year workmanship warranty on replacements, 1 year on repairs, plus the manufacturer's material warranty. In writing before we start.
